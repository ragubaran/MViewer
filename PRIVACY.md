# Privacy Policy for MDViewer

**Last Updated:** September 8, 2026

MDViewer ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use the MDViewer Chrome Extension (the "Extension").

## 1. Data Collection and Usage
**We do not collect, transmit, distribute, or sell any of your personal data.** 

MDViewer is designed to be a completely offline-first, local-only application. 
- All document parsing, rendering, and cryptographic sealing (SHA-256) happens entirely locally within your browser.
- We do not use external analytics, trackers, or telemetrics.
- We do not have servers that receive your documents, form inputs, or metadata.

## 2. Local Storage
The Extension may use your browser's local storage (such as `chrome.storage`) exclusively to save your local application preferences (e.g., Light/Dark mode themes) and temporary draft states of forms you are actively filling out. This data remains on your device and is never transmitted over the internet to us or any third parties.

## 3. Permissions
The Extension requires the following permissions to function:
- **`sidePanel`**: Allows the extension to open in the browser's side panel so you can read and fill out `.form.md` documents alongside other web pages.
- **`storage`**: Allows the extension to save your theme preferences and local form drafts on your device.

These permissions are used strictly to provide the core functionality of the Extension and are never used to track your browsing behavior or extract data from other websites.

## 4. Third-Party Services
The Extension does not integrate with any third-party data collection services or APIs.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in this file on our GitHub repository. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

## 6. Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please contact us by opening an issue on our [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/ragubaran/MViewer).
